Photos are local, size-optimised copies. The exterior image is the historical photo used on the pub’s Our Story page. The logo is the original transparent bitmap and is not redrawn.

Colours derive from the source Wix brand tokens: dark olive `32,36,26`, secondary olive `76,80,69`, warm paper `240,231,217` and gold `225,208,182`. Shared CSS custom properties hold the palette.

Typography uses locally hosted Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans, obtained from Google Fonts. Their SIL Open Font Licences are included alongside the fonts. No external font fetch or new image generation is required at runtime.
