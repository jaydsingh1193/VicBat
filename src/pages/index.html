YOUR CORNER OF BATTERSEA.

A PUB WITH PERSONALITY

A warm welcome.
For the whole gang.

A neighbourhood gastropub with a sun-kissed garden, free games and room for your four-legged friends. Drop in for a quiet pint. Settle in for the evening.

A garden for all seasons +

Long lunches and evening drinks outdoors, with umbrellas, awnings and heaters for changeable weather. Please note that not every table is covered or heated.

Dogs are part of the family +

Dogs are welcome for drinks and meals. We have water bowls, treats from the team and a relaxed atmosphere for dogs and their humans.

The games are on us +

Our games room is free to use. Enjoy pool, ping pong and foosball without needing coins or a booking. Grab a drink and get playing.

The big moments, on the big screen +

We’re a pub rather than a dedicated sports bar, but we show major terrestrial tournaments: the Rugby World Cup, Six Nations, Football World Cup, UEFA Euros, Wimbledon, Grand National and Royal Ascot. Outdoor screenings depend on the weather and aren’t guaranteed.

ROOTED IN BATTERSEA

Over a century
of “one more?”

The Victoria has been part of Queenstown Road since the early 1900s. First known as the Victoria Hotel, it stood on what was then Queen’s Road, renamed in 1944.

Our distinctive wedge-shaped building has seen a few changes, including a spell as Browns, before returning to its original name. The vintage typewriters on our walls, the back-room games and the familiar faces are all part of the story.

Today, it’s still a place for the neighbourhood to come together, from a quiet pint to a late night with friends.
